Culinary Ethos

No shortcuts. No artificial colors. Only slow craftsmanship.

Srestaurents was envisioned as an antidote to fast-casual dining. We take inspiration from the historic royal kitchens of Awadh and Hyderabad, where food was treated as an art form of aromatic balance.

Our Dal Makhani simmers uninterrupted for 24 hours on gentle charcoal. Our biryanis are sealed with stone-ground whole wheat dough to trap the steam within the clay pot. And our Pan-Asian wok dishes bring sharp, vibrant counterpoints to the rich Indian gravies.
