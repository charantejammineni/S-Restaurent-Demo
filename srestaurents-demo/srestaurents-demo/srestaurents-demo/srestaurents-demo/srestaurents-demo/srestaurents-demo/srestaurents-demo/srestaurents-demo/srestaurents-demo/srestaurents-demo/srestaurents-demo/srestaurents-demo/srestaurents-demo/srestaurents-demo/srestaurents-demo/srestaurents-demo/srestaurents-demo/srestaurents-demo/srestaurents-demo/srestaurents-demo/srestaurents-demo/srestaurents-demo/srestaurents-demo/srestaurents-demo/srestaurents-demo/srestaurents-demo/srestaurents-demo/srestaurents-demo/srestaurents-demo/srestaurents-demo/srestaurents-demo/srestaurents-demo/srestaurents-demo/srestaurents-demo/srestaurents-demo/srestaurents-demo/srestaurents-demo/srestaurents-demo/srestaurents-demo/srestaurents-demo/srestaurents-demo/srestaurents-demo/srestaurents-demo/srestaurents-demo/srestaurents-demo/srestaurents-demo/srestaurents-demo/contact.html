Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

We encourage smart-casual attire. Traditional Indian festive wear is warmly welcomed.

Do you accommodate Jain or Vegan diets?

Yes, several of our curries, dal makhani variations, and tandoor starters can be prepared strictly without onion, garlic, or dairy.

Can I bring outside celebration cakes?

We provide complimentary artisanal dessert candles with advance notice. Outside cakes are permitted with prior clearance.

How far in advance can I book a table?

Table bookings open 30 days in advance via our online reservation system.
